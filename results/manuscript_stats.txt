RESULTS PARAGRAPH 1 (NSSP):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Across states, the median fraction of RSV-associated ED visits (NSSP) occurring outside the October-March window was 5.9% (IQR: 4.7-9.6%) in 2023-2024; and 7.7% (IQR: 6.3-10.5%) in 2024-2025; and 16.4% (IQR: 13.9-22.2%) in 2025-2026.

RESULTS PARAGRAPH 2 (NHSN):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For laboratory-confirmed RSV-associated pediatric hospitalizations (NHSN, ages 0-4), the median out-of-window fraction was 5.7% (IQR: 4.5-8.5%) in 2024-2025; and 13.9% (IQR: 10.6-20.2%) in 2025-2026.

DISCUSSION PARAGRAPH:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Across 3 NSSP seasons and 2 NHSN season(s), approximately 8% of RSV activity nationally - and up to 43% in some states - occurred outside the current recommended October-March nirsevimab administration window. This pattern was consistent across seasons, suggesting that year-over-year variability does not eliminate out-of-window burden.

METHODOLOGICAL DETAILS
================================================================================

DATA SOURCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NSSP:
  - Dataset: CDC NSSP Emergency Department Visit Trajectories
  - Socrata API dataset ID: rdmq-nq56
  - Field: percent_visits_rsv (% of ED visits with RSV-related code, all ages)
  - Seasons: 2023-2024, 2024-2025, 2025-2026
  - Geographic scope: all available states plus District of Columbia; aggregate rows and territories excluded

NHSN:
  - Dataset: CDC NHSN Weekly Hospital Respiratory Data (HRD)
  - Socrata API dataset ID: ua7e-t2fy
  - Primary field: numconfrsvnewadmped0to4 (lab-confirmed RSV admissions, ages 0-4)
  - Additional strata: totalconfrsvnewadmped (all pediatric), totalconfrsvnewadm (all ages)
  - Seasons included: 2024-2025, 2025-2026
  - Completeness criterion: >=45 states reporting for 2023-24, >=40 for later seasons

BOOTSTRAP CONFIDENCE INTERVALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Nonparametric bootstrap, 10,000 replicates
  - Resample states with replacement (n = number of states per season)
  - Point estimate: median outside fraction
  - 95% CI: 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of bootstrap distribution

LONGITUDINAL CONSISTENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - State-level coefficient of variation (CV) = SD / mean across seasons
  - Spearman rank correlation of state outside-fractions between season pairs
  - Assesses whether states that are high in one season remain high in the next

INFANT PROPHYLAXIS PROTECTION MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Daily birth cohorts with births uniformly distributed across calendar days
  - Cohorts begin with infants who could be eligible at the earliest scenario window
  - Eligible infants receive prophylaxis through newborn/first-week or routine-care opportunities inside each scenario window
  - Reference assumptions: 18.5% uptake, 38.1% newborn/first-week dosing among recipients, eligibility for receipt before age 8 months, and follow-up censored at age 12 months
  - Protection assumptions: 6-day rise to peak concentration and smoothed time-varying effectiveness through day 210
  - Primary metrics: cohort-weighted median population-level fractional protection and population activity-weighted protection
  - Hospitalization translation uses the 100% uptake scenario to isolate timing effects
  - State-season RSV exposure weights come from the observed NSSP or NHSN epidemic curve
